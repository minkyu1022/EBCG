Energy-Based Classifier Guidance

Non-Gaussian SI Drift SDE

Fixed-case derivation for sampling the reward-tilted target $\dat{p}\,\bol{\pi}^{\lambda}$ with stochastic interpolant fields and a drift-SDE predictor-corrector.

$p$
data distribution. The generative model learns marginal $\dat{p_t}$, velocity $\dat{v^p_t}$, and score $\dat{s^p_t}$.
$\pi$
Boltzmann distribution $\bol{\pi}\propto e^r$, reward $r=-E/T$ from the MLIP. Flow Sampling learns marginal $\bol{\pi_t}$, velocity $\bol{v^\pi_t}$, and score $\bol{s^\pi_t}$.
$v^i_t$
marginal velocity $v^i_t=\mathbb{E}[\dot X_t\mid X_t]$ for component $i$.
$u^i_t$
SDE drift $u^i_t=v^i_t+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}s^i_t$ for the chosen sampling diffusion.
$s^i_t$
marginal score $s^i_t=\nabla\log(\cdot)_t$ for component $i$.
$\tilde s_t$
tilted score $\tilde s_t=\dat{s^p_t}+\lambda\bol{s^\pi_t}$.
$\tilde v_t$
tilt velocity $\tilde v_t=\dat{v^p_t}+\lambda\bol{v^\pi_t}$.
$\rho_t$
sampling path $\rho_t\propto \dat{p_t}\,\bol{\pi_t}^{\lambda}$.
$\lambda,\sigma_t$
tilt strength and sampling diffusion.
$\hat x_1,\widehat{\nabla r}$
learned heads: denoiser and reward-gradient head.
$t:0\to1$
$t=0$ prior $\rho_0$, $t=1$ data end.
00

Target

$$\rho_1\;\propto\;\dat{p}\,\bol{\pi}^{\lambda}\;\propto\;\dat{p}\,e^{\lambda r}.$$
$$\rho_t\;\propto\;\dat{p_t}\,\bol{\pi_t}^{\lambda},\qquad \tilde s_t=\nabla\log\rho_t=\dat{s^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{s^\pi_t}.$$
Noising does not commute with multiplication, so $\rho_t$ is not the true noised reward-tilted endpoint at intermediate $t$. It matches at $t=1$. This product-of-marginals path is the computable path because the trained component models expose $\dat{s^p_t}$ and $\bol{s^\pi_t}$.
01

Data Distribution: Stochastic Interpolant

The SI data model exposes the data velocity and score $\dat{(v^p_t,s^p_t)}$. It is trained on data endpoints, not on reward-tilted samples. No target samples from $\rho_1$ are required, and the corrector below does not require estimating a velocity divergence.

$$\dat{v^p_t(x)=\mathbb E[\dot X_t\mid X_t=x]},\qquad \dat{s^p_t(x)=\nabla\log p_t(x)}.$$

In the AdsorbGen-style SI parameterization, the stochastic interpolant and velocity target are:

$$X_t=tX_1+(1-t)X_0+\gamma_t Z,\qquad \dot X_t=X_1-X_0+\dot\gamma_t Z.$$
$$\gamma_t=0.1\sqrt{\max(t(1-t),10^{-5})},\qquad \dot\gamma_t=\frac{0.1(1-2t)}{2\sqrt{\max(t(1-t),10^{-5})}}.$$

The code-compatible form can use an $x_1$ head and convert it to the velocity used in the loss.

$$\dat{v_\theta(X_t,t)=\frac{\hat x^p_1(X_t,t)-X_t}{1-t}},\qquad \dat{\mathcal L_{\text{SI}}=\mathbb E\|v_\theta(X_t,t)-\dot X_t\|^2+\eta\,\mathbb E\|z_\theta(X_t,t)-Z\|^2}.$$
$$\dat{v^p_t(x)=v_\theta(x,t)},\qquad \dat{s^p_t(x)=-\frac{z_\theta(x,t)}{\gamma_t}}.$$

Used alone, this data model can generate from $p$ by the same drift-SDE form without reward guidance:

$$\mathrm dX=\Big[\dat{v^p_t(X)}+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\dat{s^p_t(X)}\Big]\mathrm dt+\sigma_t\,\mathrm dW,\qquad X_0\sim\rho_0.$$
02

Classifier: Flow Sampling

The Boltzmann classifier $\bol{\pi\propto e^r}$ is not trained from equilibrium samples. It is trained from the MLIP reward by Flow Sampling. With the non-Gaussian prior bridge $X_t=tX_1+(1-t)X_0$ and $g_t^2=2\gamma t$, the reward-bearing conditional drift target is:

$$\bol{u_{t\mid1}^{\pi}(X_t)=(X_1-X_0)+\gamma\,\nabla r(X_1)},\qquad \nabla r(X_1)=-\nabla E(X_1)/T.$$

Because the prior is non-Gaussian, a single drift output cannot recover both velocity and score. Flow Sampling therefore uses two heads.

$$\bol{\hat x_1^\pi(x,t)\approx\mathbb E[X_1\mid X_t=x]},\qquad \bol{\widehat{\nabla r}(x,t)\approx\mathbb E[\nabla r(X_1)\mid X_t=x]}.$$
$$\bol{\mathcal L_{\text{den}}=\mathbb E\|\hat x_1^\pi(X_t,t)-X_1\|^2},\qquad \bol{\mathcal L_{\nabla r}=\mathbb E\|\widehat{\nabla r}(X_t,t)-\nabla r(X_1)\|^2}.$$

The two learned outputs are then converted into the Boltzmann velocity and score used by the predictor-corrector.

$$\bol{v^\pi_t(x)=\frac{\hat x_1^\pi(x,t)-x}{1-t}},\qquad \bol{s^\pi_t(x)=\frac{\widehat{\nabla r}(x,t)}{t}}.$$

Used alone, this classifier sampler targets the Boltzmann marginal path $\pi_t$:

$$\mathrm dX=\Big[\bol{v^\pi_t(X)}+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\bol{s^\pi_t(X)}\Big]\mathrm dt+\sigma_t\,\mathrm dW,\qquad X_0\sim\rho_0.$$

Inside EBCG, it is not used as a standalone generator; it contributes $\lambda\bol{v^\pi_t}$ and $\lambda\bol{s^\pi_t}$ to the tilted predictor.

Why the reward appears in the score

Taking the conditional expectation of the Flow-Sampling target gives the SDE drift $u^\pi_t=v^\pi_t+\gamma\widehat{\nabla r}$. The Fokker-Planck identity gives $u^\pi_t=v^\pi_t+\tfrac{g_t^2}{2}s^\pi_t$. Since $g_t^2=2\gamma t$, matching the two expressions yields $s^\pi_t=\widehat{\nabla r}/t$.

03

Predictor

The drift-SDE predictor uses the tilt velocity, not the data velocity alone. This transport is what makes the FKC weight divergence-free.

$$\mathrm dX=\Big[\,\tilde v_t+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\,\tilde s_t\,\Big]\,\mathrm dt+\sigma_t\,\mathrm dW.$$
$$\tilde v_t=\dat{v^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{v^\pi_t},\qquad \tilde s_t=\dat{s^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{s^\pi_t}.$$
Predictor derivation
$$g_t=\langle\tilde s_t,W_t\rangle-\sum_i\beta_i\langle s^i_t,v^i_t\rangle+\nabla\!\cdot(W_t-M_t),\qquad M_t=\textstyle\sum_i\beta_i v^i_t.$$

The divergence term $\nabla\!\cdot(W_t-M_t)$ disappears only when $W_t=M_t$, i.e. when the transport is the tilt velocity. Other transports leave a velocity-divergence term in the weight and require a trace estimator.

04

Corrector

Both correctors use the same predictor. They differ in how they pin the marginal to $\rho_t$.

Langevin

$$X\;\leftarrow\;X+\delta\,\tilde s_t(X)+\sqrt{2\delta}\;\xi,\qquad \xi\sim\mathcal N(0,I).$$

The stationary distribution is $\rho_t$ because the drift is $\tilde s_t=\nabla\log\rho_t$. It is particle-wise and exact in the infinite-corrector-step limit.

Langevin invariance

Overdamped Langevin with drift $\nabla\log\rho_t$ and diffusion $\sqrt2$ has invariant density $\rho_t$. Frozen-$t$ corrector steps therefore relax particles toward the desired marginal.

FKC / SMC

The predictor uses tilt-velocity transport, so the Feynman-Kac weight has no divergence estimator and depends only on velocity-score inner products.
$$g_t=\lambda(\lambda-1)\,\langle \bol{v^\pi_t},\bol{s^\pi_t}\rangle+\lambda\big(\langle \dat{v^p_t},\bol{s^\pi_t}\rangle+\langle \bol{v^\pi_t},\dat{s^p_t}\rangle\big).$$
$$w_t^{(k)}=\!\int_{t_{\min}}^{t}\! g_s\big(x^{(k)}_s\big)\,\mathrm ds,\qquad \mathbb{E}_{\rho_t}[\phi]\approx\sum_k\frac{e^{w_t^{(k)}}}{\sum_j e^{w_t^{(j)}}}\,\phi\big(x^{(k)}_t\big).$$
Resample only on the active interval $[t_{\min},t_{\max}]$, only within fixed atom-count bins, and temper or clip weights for large $\lambda$ because $\|s^\pi_t\|$ enters quadratically.
PDE matching derivation

Step 1, each marginal.

$$\partial_t\log(\cdot)^i_t=-\langle s^i_t,v^i_t\rangle-\nabla\!\cdot v^i_t.$$

Step 2, target path. Exponents $(\beta_p,\beta_\pi)=(1,\lambda)$, with the normalizer removed by mean subtraction.

$$B:=\partial_t\log\rho_t\big|_{\text{non-norm}}=-\sum_i\beta_i\langle s^i_t,v^i_t\rangle-\nabla\!\cdot M_t,\qquad M_t=\textstyle\sum_i\beta_i v^i_t.$$

Step 3, SDE rate. SDE drift $A_t=M_t+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\tilde s_t$, diffusion $\sigma_t$.

$$A=-\langle\tilde s_t,A_t\rangle-\nabla\!\cdot A_t+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\big(\Delta\log\rho_t+\|\tilde s_t\|^2\big).$$

After substituting $A_t$, all $\sigma_t^2$ and Laplacian terms cancel: $A=-\langle\tilde s_t,M_t\rangle-\nabla\!\cdot M_t$.

Step 4, residual weight.

$$g_t=B-A=\langle\tilde s_t,M_t\rangle-\sum_i\beta_i\langle s^i_t,v^i_t\rangle.$$

Expanding $\langle\tilde s_t,M_t\rangle=\sum_{i,j}\beta_i\beta_j\langle s^j_t,v^i_t\rangle$ and setting $(\beta_p,\beta_\pi)=(1,\lambda)$ gives the boxed FKC weight. The diagonal data term vanishes because $\beta_p(\beta_p-1)=0$.

05

Generation Algorithm

Input. Learned $\dat{(v^p,s^p)}$, learned $\bol{(v^\pi,s^\pi)}$, tilt $\lambda$, steps $N$, diffusion $\sigma_t$, and corrector choice.

  1. Train the Boltzmann classifier by Flow Sampling: regress $\bol{\hat x_1^\pi}$ and $\bol{\widehat{\nabla r}}$, then read $\bol{v^\pi_t}$ and $\bol{s^\pi_t=\widehat{\nabla r}/t}$.
  2. Train the SI data model to expose $\dat{v^p_t}$ and $\dat{s^p_t}$.
  3. Sample the prior: $X\sim\rho_0$.
  4. Evaluate heads and assemble $\tilde v_t=\dat{v^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{v^\pi_t}$ and $\tilde s_t=\dat{s^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{s^\pi_t}$.
  5. Predictor step: $X\mathrel{+}=[\tilde v_t+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\tilde s_t]\Delta t+\sigma_t\sqrt{\Delta t}\xi$.
  6. At frozen $t$, apply the chosen corrector. Langevin: $X\mathrel{+}=\delta\,\tilde s_t(X)+\sqrt{2\delta}\xi$. FKC: $w\mathrel{+}=g_t(X)\Delta t$, then resample inside fixed-$N$ bins on $[t_{\min},t_{\max}]$.
  7. Advance $t$ to $1$ and return $X\sim\rho_1\propto \dat{p}\,\bol{\pi}^{\lambda}$.
