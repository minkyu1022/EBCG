Energy-Based Classifier Guidance

Non-Gaussian SI Drift SDE

Fixed-case derivation for sampling the reward-tilted target $\dat{p}\,\bol{\pi}^{\lambda}$ with stochastic interpolant experts and a drift-SDE predictor-corrector.

$p$
data distribution. The generative model learns marginal $\dat{p_t}$, velocity $\dat{u^p_t}$, and score $\dat{s^p_t}$.
$\pi$
Boltzmann distribution $\bol{\pi}\propto e^r$, reward $r=-E/T$ from the MLIP. Flow Sampling learns marginal $\bol{\pi_t}$, velocity $\bol{u^\pi_t}$, and score $\bol{s^\pi_t}$.
$u^i_t$
expert velocity $u^i_t=\mathbb{E}[\dot X_t\mid X_t]$.
$s^i_t$
expert score $s^i_t=\nabla\log(\cdot)_t$.
$\tilde s_t$
tilted score $\tilde s_t=\dat{s^p_t}+\lambda\bol{s^\pi_t}$.
$\tilde u_t$
tilt velocity $\tilde u_t=\dat{u^p_t}+\lambda\bol{u^\pi_t}$.
$\rho_t$
sampling path $\rho_t\propto \dat{p_t}\,\bol{\pi_t}^{\lambda}$.
$\lambda,\sigma_t$
tilt strength and sampling diffusion.
$\hat x_1,\widehat{\nabla r}$
learned heads: denoiser and reward-gradient head.
$t:0\to1$
$t=0$ prior $\rho_0$, $t=1$ data end.
00

Target

$$\rho_1\;\propto\;\dat{p}\,\bol{\pi}^{\lambda}\;\propto\;\dat{p}\,e^{\lambda r}.$$
$$\rho_t\;\propto\;\dat{p_t}\,\bol{\pi_t}^{\lambda},\qquad \tilde s_t=\nabla\log\rho_t=\dat{s^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{s^\pi_t}.$$
Noising does not commute with multiplication, so $\rho_t$ is not the true noised reward-tilted endpoint at intermediate $t$. It matches at $t=1$. This product-of-marginals path is the computable path because the trained experts expose $\dat{s^p_t}$ and $\bol{s^\pi_t}$.
01

Models

The SI data model exposes $\dat{(u^p_t,s^p_t)}$. The Flow-Sampling Boltzmann model exposes $\bol{(u^\pi_t,s^\pi_t)}$ through its two heads. No target samples from $\rho_1$ are required, and the corrector below does not require estimating a velocity divergence.

$$\dat{u^p_t(x)=\mathbb E[\dot X_t\mid X_t=x]},\qquad \dat{s^p_t(x)=\nabla\log p_t(x)}.$$
$$\bol{s^\pi_t=\widehat{\nabla r}/t},\qquad \bol{u^\pi_t=v^\pi_t+\tfrac{g_t^2}{2}s^\pi_t}.$$
02

Predictor

The drift-SDE predictor uses the tilt velocity, not the data velocity alone. This transport is what makes the FKC weight divergence-free.

$$\mathrm dX=\Big[\,\tilde u_t+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\,\tilde s_t\,\Big]\,\mathrm dt+\sigma_t\,\mathrm dW.$$
$$\tilde u_t=\dat{u^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{u^\pi_t},\qquad \tilde s_t=\dat{s^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{s^\pi_t}.$$
Predictor derivation
$$g_t=\langle\tilde s_t,W_t\rangle-\sum_i\beta_i\langle s^i_t,u^i_t\rangle+\nabla\!\cdot(W_t-U_t),\qquad U_t=\textstyle\sum_i\beta_i u^i_t.$$

The divergence term $\nabla\!\cdot(W_t-U_t)$ disappears only when $W_t=U_t$, i.e. when the transport is the tilt velocity. Other transports leave a velocity-divergence term in the weight and require a trace estimator.

03

Corrector

Both correctors use the same predictor. They differ in how they pin the marginal to $\rho_t$.

Langevin

$$X\;\leftarrow\;X+\delta\,\tilde s_t(X)+\sqrt{2\delta}\;\xi,\qquad \xi\sim\mathcal N(0,I).$$

The stationary distribution is $\rho_t$ because the drift is $\tilde s_t=\nabla\log\rho_t$. It is particle-wise and exact in the infinite-corrector-step limit.

Langevin invariance

Overdamped Langevin with drift $\nabla\log\rho_t$ and diffusion $\sqrt2$ has invariant density $\rho_t$. Frozen-$t$ corrector steps therefore relax particles toward the desired marginal.

FKC / SMC

The predictor uses tilt-velocity transport, so the Feynman-Kac weight has no divergence estimator and depends only on velocity-score inner products.
$$g_t=\lambda(\lambda-1)\,\langle \bol{u^\pi_t},\bol{s^\pi_t}\rangle+\lambda\big(\langle \dat{u^p_t},\bol{s^\pi_t}\rangle+\langle \bol{u^\pi_t},\dat{s^p_t}\rangle\big).$$
$$w_t^{(k)}=\!\int_{t_{\min}}^{t}\! g_s\big(x^{(k)}_s\big)\,\mathrm ds,\qquad \mathbb{E}_{\rho_t}[\phi]\approx\sum_k\frac{e^{w_t^{(k)}}}{\sum_j e^{w_t^{(j)}}}\,\phi\big(x^{(k)}_t\big).$$
Resample only on the active interval $[t_{\min},t_{\max}]$, only within fixed atom-count bins, and temper or clip weights for large $\lambda$ because $\|s^\pi_t\|$ enters quadratically.
PDE matching derivation

Step 1, each marginal.

$$\partial_t\log(\cdot)^i_t=-\langle s^i_t,u^i_t\rangle-\nabla\!\cdot u^i_t.$$

Step 2, target path. Exponents $(\beta_p,\beta_\pi)=(1,\lambda)$, with the normalizer removed by mean subtraction.

$$B:=\partial_t\log\rho_t\big|_{\text{non-norm}}=-\sum_i\beta_i\langle s^i_t,u^i_t\rangle-\nabla\!\cdot U_t,\qquad U_t=\textstyle\sum_i\beta_i u^i_t.$$

Step 3, SDE rate. Drift $V_t=U_t+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\tilde s_t$, diffusion $\sigma_t$.

$$A=-\langle\tilde s_t,V_t\rangle-\nabla\!\cdot V_t+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\big(\Delta\log\rho_t+\|\tilde s_t\|^2\big).$$

After substituting $V_t$, all $\sigma_t^2$ and Laplacian terms cancel: $A=-\langle\tilde s_t,U_t\rangle-\nabla\!\cdot U_t$.

Step 4, residual weight.

$$g_t=B-A=\langle\tilde s_t,U_t\rangle-\sum_i\beta_i\langle s^i_t,u^i_t\rangle.$$

Expanding $\langle\tilde s_t,U_t\rangle=\sum_{i,j}\beta_i\beta_j\langle s^j_t,u^i_t\rangle$ and setting $(\beta_p,\beta_\pi)=(1,\lambda)$ gives the boxed FKC weight. The diagonal data term vanishes because $\beta_p(\beta_p-1)=0$.

04

Generation Algorithm

Input. Learned $\dat{(u^p,s^p)}$, learned $\bol{(u^\pi,s^\pi)}$, tilt $\lambda$, steps $N$, diffusion $\sigma_t$, and corrector choice.

  1. Sample the prior: $X\sim\rho_0$.
  2. Evaluate heads and assemble $\tilde u_t=\dat{u^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{u^\pi_t}$ and $\tilde s_t=\dat{s^p_t}+\lambda\,\bol{s^\pi_t}$.
  3. Predictor step: $X\mathrel{+}=[\tilde u_t+\tfrac{\sigma_t^2}{2}\tilde s_t]\Delta t+\sigma_t\sqrt{\Delta t}\xi$.
  4. At frozen $t$, apply the chosen corrector. Langevin: $X\mathrel{+}=\delta\,\tilde s_t(X)+\sqrt{2\delta}\xi$. FKC: $w\mathrel{+}=g_t(X)\Delta t$, then resample inside fixed-$N$ bins on $[t_{\min},t_{\max}]$.
  5. Advance $t$ to $1$ and return $X\sim\rho_1\propto \dat{p}\,\bol{\pi}^{\lambda}$.
