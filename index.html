Energy-based Classifier Guidance

Interactive derivation reference for reward-tilted sampling.

Setup

  • Linear schedule: $\alpha_t=t$, $\sigma_t=1-t$, and $X_t=tX_1+(1-t)X_0$.
  • Flow-Sampling diffusion: assume $g_t^2=2\gamma t$, so $\dfrac{g_t^2}{2\alpha_t}=\gamma$.
  • Prior: $X_0\sim\pi$. Boltzmann model: $\mu\propto e^r$ with reward $r(x)=-E(x)/T$.
  • Data model: $q$ fits $p_{\text{data}}$ directly, or $p\cdot q$ fits the residual so $p_\theta q_\phi=p_{\text{data}}$.

The reward-tilted target distribution is $\rho_1^\lambda\propto p_{\text{data}}\,\mu^\lambda\propto p_{\text{data}}\,e^{\lambda r}$. Its pathwise tilted score is $\tilde s_t=s_t^{\text{data}}+\lambda s_t^p$.

Connection to classifier guidance: standard diffusion guidance adds a classifier score $\nabla_x\log p(y\mid x_t)$ to a base score. Here the Boltzmann sampler $p$ supplies the guidance score $s_t^p$ from energy/reward, while $q$ supplies the data velocity or score. Connection to classifier-free guidance: CFG combines conditional and unconditional model outputs; EBCG similarly combines a data model and an energy-guidance model, but the guidance term is explicit $\lambda s_t^p$ and targets $p_{\text{data}}\mu^\lambda$.
